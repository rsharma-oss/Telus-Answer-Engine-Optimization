07

Methodology, for the room

The panel. 20 realistic Canadian-telco buying questions asked every Monday of 5 AI systems — ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI Overview, Google AI Mode. Results appended to a durable, append-only tracking store. 13 weekly pulls on record.

What "share of answer" means. % of AI responses that contain a substantive mention or citation of the brand, within the measured window. Scores directly comparable across brands and across time.

How this differs from brand-tracker research. A brand tracker asks humans what they remember. This measures what the machine layer recommends to a real buyer in the moment of decision. The two correlate, but the answer-layer read leads — it's a leading indicator for the tracker.

What this scorecard is not. Not an SEO ranking report. Not a media-monitoring feed. Not a predictive model. It is a weekly measurement of which brands and narratives the AI assistants surface to Canadian buyers — treated as brand-equity input for the CMO's executive team.
