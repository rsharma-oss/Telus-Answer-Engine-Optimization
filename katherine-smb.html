Three issues to close before Q4 26

Each closable with a product/merchandising move on the TELUS side and a matching content presence intervention on ours. Numbers show current share-of-answer and plausible ceiling if closed.
1 · Home-office wireline has no TELUS story in the AI answer
10 → target 45
Buy best wireline solution for my home office setup.
Cited sources are Reddit r/canadianISPs, PlanHub comparison pages, and Bell's business fiber pages. No packaged "Home Office" bundle SKU shows up. TELUS Business Internet is present but not merchandised for the home-office buyer.
The play Package and merchandise a TELUS Home Office Fiber bundle — priority service, business-grade uptime, one-line billing. Content on telus.com/business, Reddit r/canadianISPs presence, WhistleOut & PlanHub listing updates. Expected lift: 40–50 within 6 weeks of consistent presence.
2 · Flex / variable data isn't a named TELUS product
8 → target 40
Are there flexible mobile data plans I can adjust monthly based on usage?
Freedom Mobile and Rogers Infinite are the most-cited answers. Bell shows up on business-flex language. TELUS doesn't have a product name the AI can index.
The play Launch or reposition a named TELUS Business Flex Data plan. Front-load PR + comparison-site coverage. Expected lift: 30–40 within one measurement cycle after content lands.
3 · SMB Essentials has real momentum — accelerate it
35 → target 65
My small business needs a cost-effective wireless solution with unlimited data.
TELUS is already in the answer 40% of the time — up from 32 in July. Rogers, Bell, and Public Mobile take the rest. The SMB Essentials tier isn't consistently cited by name.
The play Sharpen SMB Essentials naming and pricing clarity; refresh WhistleOut / PlanHub listings; seed a Reddit thread with a TELUS Business rep. Expected lift: 60–65 within 8 weeks.

Methodology, for the room

The panel. 20 realistic Canadian-telco buying questions asked every Monday of 5 AI systems — ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI Overview, Google AI Mode. Results appended to a durable, append-only tracking store. Running since July 17, 2026 — 14 weekly pulls on record.

The SMB slice. 5 of the 20 questions are SMB-relevant: home-office wireline, flexible mobile data, cost-effective SMB wireless, business-owner sentiment on TELUS, rural-business reliability. This scorecard filters the panel to those five; the aggregate is a simple unweighted average.

Share-of-answer. % of AI responses in which the brand appears as a substantive mention or citation, within the measured window. A score of 40 on a question means TELUS appears in 40% of the responses to that question.
